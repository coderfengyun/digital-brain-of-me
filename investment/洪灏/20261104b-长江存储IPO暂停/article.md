# 长江存储 IPO 暂停与市场底部信号

**作者**：洪灏  
**发布时间**：2026-11-04 22:30

## 原文

Was told that the YMTC IPO would be suspended due to market volatility. IPO suspension is often considered a sign of market bottom.

Quoting my Nov 2 post: brokers buy stocks, IPO suspended without official announcement, and no large shareholders selling after IPO. These measures are necessary to cut liquidity outflows.

## 中文翻译

听说长江存储（YMTC）的 IPO 将因市场波动而暂停。IPO 暂停通常被视为市场接近底部的一个信号。

正如我 11 月 2 日的帖子所说：券商买入股票、IPO 在未正式宣布的情况下暂停，以及 IPO 后大股东不再减持。这些措施对于减少市场流动性外流是必要的。

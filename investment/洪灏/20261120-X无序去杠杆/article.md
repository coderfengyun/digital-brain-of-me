## English original

After a brief but strong rebound in late October, Chinese stocks continue to plunge, against the backdrop of heavy national-team intervention, ultra-low-interest-rate rediscount loans for stock repurchases, and repeated jawboning by regulators.

This round of heavy selloff recalls the quant quake in early 2024. The sudden and seemingly incessant rounds of selloff out of the blue are concerning, but they offer clues of the real cause of this plunge.

It has the trademark of disorderly deleveraging. If we look at the mainland market's margin debt balance, it has returned to the August 2025 level and decreased by one third. But it is still more than 50% higher than the level after the quant quake in early 2024. We have found the culprit behind the selloff, but it is still at large.

The surge in semiconductors this year is nothing short of spectacular. One leading optical-module company went from 20 to 1,500 in less than a year. It is now down almost 80%, but remains roughly 40 times above where it took off. It is the poster child of the semiconductor bubble.

When we said the semiconductor bubble was about to burst in mid-June, few listened and more chided. The memory is still fresh from 2015, when we called the ChiNext bubble. Right now, the crowd is incredibly bearish. While we think the pressure will linger, we should not join the bearish chorus. It is the essential quality of a contrarian.

## 中文整理

10 月底短暂强劲反弹后，中国股市在国家队干预、低息回购增持再贷款与监管表态并存的环境中继续下跌。作者认为本轮抛售具有 2024 年初“量化地震”式的无序去杠杆特征：两融余额已回到 2025 年 8 月水平并较高点下降约三分之一，但仍比 2024 年初量化冲击后的水平高 50% 以上，说明去杠杆压力尚未清除。

半导体泡沫是典型案例：某光模块龙头一年内由约 20 涨至 1,500，随后虽下跌近 80%，仍较起点高约 40 倍。作者判断压力仍会持续，但在市场情绪已经极度悲观时，不应机械加入看空共识；逆向投资的关键是既承认出清未完，也避免在情绪极端时追空。

## 原文

Li Qiang's State Council meeting emphasized strengthening the market stabilization scheme. It was the odd one out among welfare issues, showing the importance of market stability as the year draws to an end.

My hypothesis is that the growth target is largely within reach, and the government's focus is shifting to market stability. The national team continued buying broad ETFs, albeit at a slower pace. Brokers used the third round of rediscount loans to buy stocks. Many semiconductor names were bid despite the Asian selloff. Despite a five-sigma selloff in the US, Chinese assets remained resilient.

## 中文翻译

李强主持的国务院会议强调要强化市场稳定机制。它在一系列民生议题中显得格外突出，说明临近年末，市场稳定的重要性正在上升。

我的假设是，全年增长目标已经大体可达，政府注意力因此转向稳定市场。国家队仍在买入宽基 ETF，尽管速度有所放缓；券商使用第三轮再贷款资金买入股票；即使亚洲市场普遍下跌，许多半导体股票仍有买盘。面对美国市场五倍标准差级别的抛售，中国资产仍表现出韧性。

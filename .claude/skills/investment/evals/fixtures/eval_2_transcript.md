## 英文转录

Host: The enthusiasm really is with the AI trade. But I want to know, how is it the same or different than the huge rally that we're seeing play out in the Korean markets?

Hao Hong: I would say that the small indices in China that is tracking the tech sector more in China is closely correlated with the KOSPI. And also the semiconductor names is getting valuation from the Korean peer as well. So the saying in the market really, as long as the SK Hynix is going up, then the semiconductor sector is going to go up. But this is a different ecosystem that is being built out in China.

Host: At what point does this parabolic move become unsustainable?

Hao Hong: Well, I think every day we keep soul searching, asking ourselves, when is this thing going to end? Because we know that this is a bubble. There's no other way to put this. Despite very strong earnings flows this year in the tech sector, we saw a similar move in the 2000 tech bubble as well. The blessing for this round is that the valuation is still not as high as when it was in March 2000. Back then it was like 50 plus times. And now we are like 40 plus times. So if you use the March 2000 as a benchmark, then we have another 20, 25% to go.

Host: What are you expecting from the Trump-Xi summit?

Hao Hong: I think the market would be continuing to be supported with flow of good news. The RMB appreciation and also rare earth and also tech cooperation would be very high on the agenda. We should be able to see 6.5 this year. And in the longer term, we should see the yuan breaking 5 handle.

Host: In terms of China's energy security, how critical is it to your investment thesis?

Hao Hong: I think everyone has been surprised by the fact that the market is immune to very high oil price. Because of the very fast new energy revolution in China in the past couple of years, the imported oil as a percentage of total oil consumption has decreased from 50 to 20 percent. And also China has like over a billion barrels of oil in reserve. So that put China in a better position than the Asian peers.

## 中文翻译

主持人：市场热情集中在AI交易上。这与韩国市场的大涨有何异同？

洪灏：中国追踪科技板块的小型指数与KOSPI高度相关。半导体股票的估值也在从韩国同行获得参照。市场的说法是，只要SK海力士在涨，半导体板块就会涨。但中国在构建一个不同的生态系统。

主持人：这种抛物线式上涨何时变得不可持续？

洪灏：我觉得我们每天都在拷问自己，这东西什么时候会结束？因为我们知道这是泡沫，没有其他说法。尽管今年科技板块盈利增长强劲，但2000年科技泡沫时也有类似走势。这一轮的好处是估值还没到2000年3月那么高——那时50多倍，现在40多倍。以2000年3月为基准，还有20-25%上涨空间。

主持人：你对特习峰会有什么期待？

洪灏：我认为市场将继续受到好消息流的支撑。人民币升值、稀土和科技合作将是重点议程。今年应该能看到6.5。长期来看，人民币将突破5字头。

主持人：中国的能源安全对你的投资论点有多关键？

洪灏：每个人都对市场对高油价免疫感到惊讶。由于中国过去几年新能源革命的快速发展，进口石油占总消费比例从50%降到了20%。而且中国有超过十亿桶石油储备，这让中国比亚洲同侪处于更有利的位置。
